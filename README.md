# Hi, I'm Max Kopp

**Bioelectronics Researcher | Inventor | Incoming Rice University '30**

I build nanomaterial-based biosensors for non-invasive health monitoring. My work spans optical polarimetry, machine learning for sensor calibration, and wearable device design.

## Research

- 2 peer-reviewed publications ([Biosensors & Bioelectronics](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bios.2025), [Applied and Computational Engineering](https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/72))
- 3 U.S. patent filings including 1 non-provisional utility patent
- S.T. Yau Science Award Bronze Medal (Physics, North America Top 3)
- Presented to the Chief of Naval Research; recognized by ONR and AFRL
